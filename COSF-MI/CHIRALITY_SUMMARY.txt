================================================================================
KEY CONCEPTS
================================================================================

SPIRAL HANDEDNESS:
  Right-handed (+1): Clockwise spiral, positive concepts
  Left-handed (-1): Counter-clockwise spiral, negative concepts

PERSPECTIVE SHIFTING:
  Same word, different context = different chirality
  Example: "expensive"
    - Luxury context: RIGHT-handed (positive)
    - Burden context: LEFT-handed (negative)

GEOMETRIC SEMANTICS:
  Meaning encoded through GEOMETRY, not just weights
  Opposite chirality = orthogonal in phase space

TERAHERTZ SCALING:
  Frequencies scaled to 10^12 Hz for future spintronic hardware
